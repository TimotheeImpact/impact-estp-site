Trained as an architect and passionate about 3D since his student days, Emmanuel Di Giacomo worked in multimedia at Flammarion, then in software publishing at Abvent.

He joined Autodesk in 2002 to launch Revit in France. More than twenty years later, he is Head of BIM Ecosystem Development for Europe at Autodesk. He supports major construction companies, design firms, professional federations and schools through the digital revolution in construction: BIM, artificial intelligence, data and collaboration.

## In this interview

- From the first 3D computers at architecture school to Flammarion's CD-ROMs: a career guided by 3D
- 2002: launching Revit in France, "revise it"?
- Digital model or BIM? Why the "I" changes everything
- Notre-Dame de Paris: building a 3D model
- From 3D to 8D
- Paris 2024, Grand Paris, Notre-Dame: Revit on iconic projects
- AI assistants and MCP servers: talking to your model in plain language
- Training: what young engineers and architects in France still lack
- Mastering your trade and digital tools: his advice to avoid being disrupted
