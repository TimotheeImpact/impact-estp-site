Emmanuel Bordignon is an ESTP graduate with 25 years of experience in real estate investment between Paris, London and Milan.

Having worked for major groups such as Cushman & Wakefield and Deutsche Bank, he is now CEO of Costruire Group, a long-established construction company on Lake Maggiore that he is turning into a real estate developer.

## In this interview

- An expat's career
- "Location, location, location": the golden rule of investment explained
- Financial arbitrage, opportunistic funds and *special situations*: how to create value
- Why leave the big funds to take over an SME
- The art of delegating and working at your "first level of incompetence"
- Mentors, failures and boldness: his advice for future engineers
