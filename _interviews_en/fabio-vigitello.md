*Interview in Italian.*

Fabio Vigitello is General Manager of Villa e Palazzo Aminta in Stresa, the only hotel on the Italian side of Lake Maggiore that is a member of The Leading Hotels of the World. He has more than 20 years of experience between Italy and China.

Born into a family of hoteliers, he started out in Food & Beverage and went on to open and run five-star establishments in Italy and abroad, including the first Italian-managed hotel in China. Since 2017, he has run Villa e Palazzo Aminta, a historic residence more than a hundred years old, which he brings to life with care, vision and innovation.

## In this interview

- From Food & Beverage to management: an international career
- What luxury guests on Lake Maggiore are looking for today
- The Leading Hotels of the World: what it takes to become a member
- Lake Maggiore vs Lake Como: a similar product, a different reputation
- Sustainability, much more than linen and disposables
- The hotel of the future, increasingly tailor-made
- Engineers in luxury, the sacrifices of the job and his advice for future hospitality talent
