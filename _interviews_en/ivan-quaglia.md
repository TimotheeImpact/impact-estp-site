*Interview in Italian.*

Ivan Quaglia is an expert in luxury hospitality and experiential tourism, with more than 20 years of experience between the Maldives, Dubai, the Bahamas and Lake Maggiore.

A former executive for major international brands such as One&Only Resorts, he has led five-star repositionings, such as the Gioia Resorts in the Maldives for Valtur, and spent ten years working to raise Lake Maggiore's profile internationally.

## In this interview

- His international career
- Five-star repositioning: the logistical and strategic challenges of a resort in the Maldives
- Lake Maggiore vs Lake Como: the "George Clooney" effect
- The new face of luxury and experiential tourism
- Artificial intelligence and sustainability in hospitality
- How the job market is changing, the sacrifices of the job and his advice for future talent
