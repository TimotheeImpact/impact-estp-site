Maxime Gueguin holds a PhD in structural mechanics from the École des Ponts and the French agrégation in civil engineering. Formerly scientific director at Eurobios Mews Labs, he is now CTO and co-founder of Simvia, a deeptech startup based in Palaiseau that democratises open-source numerical simulation for engineers.

## In this interview

- The journey of a researcher turned deeptech entrepreneur
- The finite element method explained simply
- Code_aster and open source in mechanical simulation
- Surrogate models and machine learning applied to engineering
- Simvia: democratising numerical simulation for engineers
- AsterLab: the code_aster community relaunched in 2025
- Digital twins, AI and the future of simulation in construction
- Doing a PhD in 2026: for whom, why and how?
- His advice for creating a deeptech startup in France
