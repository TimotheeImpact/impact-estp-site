Trained as a geographer and urban planner, Christophe Bocquin built his career between the public sector and property development, including ten years at Groupe Duval.

Since 2023, he has been Managing Director of S3D-SPAD, the development companies (a semi-public company and a local public company) of the Dunkirk urban area. He leads the transformation of a region in the middle of reindustrialisation: 20,000 jobs being created, a target of 1,000 new homes a year and whole districts to reinvent, such as the Pointe des Alliés.
